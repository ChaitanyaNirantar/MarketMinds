Harbor Yoga — fictional business brief for demonstration

Services: Harbor Yoga offers beginner yoga classes for adults in the local neighborhood. Introductory classes require advance booking. Each class has a maximum capacity of twelve participants.

Audience: First-time adult students who prefer calm instruction and an approachable environment. Many prospects feel intimidated by unfamiliar poses. They want to understand what to expect before booking.

Brand voice: Warm, clear, welcoming, and practical. Explain the experience without promising health benefits.

Restrictions: Do not advertise discounts or guaranteed health outcomes. Prices are not provided in this brief. Instructor availability for video filming has not been confirmed. No testimonials or previous campaign results have been supplied.

Goal context: We want to increase introductory bookings while staying within our class capacity. Useful experiments could compare explanations of a first visit with introductions to the teaching approach. These are proposed experiments, not proven results.
